The Challenge

The founder was stuck in the weeds.

Constantly busy, constantly frustrated, working overtime to hold 30+ client accounts together, with no time left to actually grow the business.

Underneath it, three operational gaps

  • Deliverables were slipping across accounts, with no shared system catching it early.
  • No accountability structure. Work fell between team members with no clear owner.
  • Clients were noticing. Several were unhappy, and delivery kept running late.

My Approach

Audit before action.

Before changing anything, I mapped how the business actually ran, not how it was assumed to run.

01

Audited the system

Mapped every tool, tracker, and process actually in use, account by account.

02

Talked to the team

Individual conversations to hear, firsthand, where work was breaking down.

03

Talked to the clients

Direct conversations on what they were, and weren't, seeing from their side.

04

Talked to leadership

Compared what the founder believed they saw against what actually existed.

The Leak

No single system tied any of it together. Not for the team, not for clients, not for the founder.

The Build

Six moves, one operating system.

01

Standardizing Project Management

One standard, deployed across every account.

Every deliverable given a clear owner, a priority, and a due date, in one place, for all 30+ accounts.

Every task carries an owner, a priority, and a date
Image Placeholder ClickUp project template img-01-clickup-template.png · 16:9 · 1600×900
02

Rebuilding the Team Cadence

Making it feel different this time.

The team had tried a shared tracker before and abandoned it within weeks, so the first job was making this one stick.

A rhythm the team could actually keep
  • Weekly team meetingsA standing weekly review of status across every client account, together, in one room.
  • Individual check-insOne-on-ones with every team member to hear, directly, where their work was breaking down.
03

Setting Up SOPs

Quality stopped depending on who happened to be doing it.

Documented the repeatable work so output became a property of the system, not the person.

Three core workflows, documented end to end
  • Documented core workflowsTurned the standardized process into written SOPs for onboarding, delivery, and reporting.
  • Consistent qualityEvery account followed the same steps, so output no longer varied by who ran it.
  • Faster ramp-upNew and existing team members had one clear reference instead of tribal knowledge.
04

KPIs for Every Team Member

A measurable definition of what good looks like.

Each person got a clear, role-level target tied to their actual responsibilities.

On-time delivery rate, the target set for every account manager.
  • Role-level targetsSpecific, measurable KPIs for each team member, tied to their actual responsibilities.
  • Visible accountabilityPerformance became something the team and the founder could see, not guess at.
05

Centralizing Reporting

The founder stopped having to ask.

Rolled account-level status into one dashboard the founder could check on their own, no longer dependent on chasing anyone for an update.

One view, self-serve, no chasing required
Image Placeholder ClickUp leadership dashboard img-02-clickup-dashboard.png · 16:9 · 1600×900
06

Automating the Repeatable Work

Removing the busywork holding the team back.

With structure, SOPs, and reporting in place, the last step was cutting the manual coordination out of the day.

Hand-offs stopped waiting on someone to remember
  • Freed-up capacityManual coordination work was cut, giving the team more time for direct client work.
  • Faster responseRecurring updates and hand-offs stopped waiting on someone to remember to send them.
